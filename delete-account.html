Delete Your Account

If you need your account or associated data removed, please send your request via email or phone.

How To Request Deletion

Email info@godselectric.com with the subject line Delete Account Request.

Include your full name, the email address or phone number associated with your account, and any relevant details.

If we need more information to verify the request, we will contact you before processing it.

Once verified, we will permanently remove your account record, history, uploads, and all associated data.
